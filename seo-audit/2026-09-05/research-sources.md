# Research sources

Reviewed 2026-09-05. Guidance is distinct from measurements of this site.

- [current first-party guidance](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/fundamentals/ai-optimization-guide): Search crawlability, indexability and useful supported content remain prerequisites for AI search visibility; eligibility does not guarantee inclusion.
- [current first-party guidance](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/essentials/technical): Accessible successful responses and indexable content are technical prerequisites, not indexing guarantees.
- [current first-party guidance](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/crawling-indexing/consolidate-duplicate-urls): Keep internal links, sitemap entries and canonical declarations consistent.
- [current first-party guidance](https://developers.google.com/search/blog/2025/05/succeeding-in-ai-search): Useful primary content and structured data aligned with visible content matter; avoid invented special AI markup.
- [current first-party guidance](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/fundamentals/using-gen-ai-content): Generated content needs real user value; repetitive keyword expansion is not a substitute.
- [current first-party guidance](https://www.bing.com/webmasters/help/ai-performance-9f8e7d6c): AI citation reporting requires authenticated data and must not be equated with rankings or traffic.
- [current first-party guidance](https://www.bing.com/webmasters/help/webmaster-guidelines-30fba23a): Maintain discoverable, useful, accurately described content.
- [current first-party guidance](https://developers.openai.com/api/docs/bots): OAI-SearchBot search access is independent of GPTBot training policy; ChatGPT-User represents user-triggered requests.
- [current first-party guidance](https://docs.perplexity.ai/docs/resources/perplexity-crawlers): PerplexityBot supports search; verified IP and user-agent together matter for WAF diagnosis. A spoofed user-agent probe cannot prove real crawler access.
- [current first-party vocabulary](https://schema.org/SoftwareSourceCode): Source-code entities can describe repository-backed software/agent contracts; validate against the visible entity and distinguish vocabulary validity from rich-result eligibility.
- [current first-party vocabulary](https://schema.org/BlogPosting): Author describes an actual Person or Organization. Omitting an unknown placeholder avoids asserting an invented person; no alternative author is inferred.

Public search observations appear in search-rankings.csv. No inference is promoted to a measured ranking or citation.
