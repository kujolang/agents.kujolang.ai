Slack installation is in private testing. General Commander and VideoOps Producer have completed a live Slack handoff with their own names and catalog portraits.

## How setup will work

1. Pick an agent and choose Slack on its profile.
2. Approve the app in Slack.
3. Open your channel and mention the agent to start. Slack may ask you to add the bot to that channel first.

## How models will work

The proposed default is to use the workspace’s configured model without asking each installer to pick one. A workspace administrator can configure a supported model account or connect a compatible local runtime. Agents can share that workspace setting or use a different model when their role calls for it.
